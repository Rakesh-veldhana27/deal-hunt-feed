Privacy Policy — Deal Hunt

App: Deal Hunt (com.lux.dealhunt)
Developer: Rahul Veldhana
Last updated: November 14, 2026

Deal Hunt is a simple deals browser. It shows a curated list of shopping deals and lets you save the ones you like. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not create accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

How the app works

  • The app downloads a public list of deals over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection from a public feed hosted on GitHub. When your device makes that request, GitHub may record it in its normal server logs (which can include your IP address). That logging is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement, not by this app — the app itself keeps no record of it.
  • Deal photos are loaded from the deal sources' servers when you view them.
  • Tapping a deal opens it on the merchant's website in your browser. The merchant's website has its own privacy policy; this app does not control it.

Data stored on your device

Deals you save (bookmark) are stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage. They are never sent to any server. Unsaving a deal removes it; uninstalling the app removes everything.

Data sharing

The app shares no data with any third party, because it collects no data in the first place.

Permissions

The app requests only two Android permissions: internet access (to fetch the deals feed) and network-state access (to know whether the device is online). Neither permission gives the app access to your files, contacts, location, or any other personal data.

Children

The app is for general audiences and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
