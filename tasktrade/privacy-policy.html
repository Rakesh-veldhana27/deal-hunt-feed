TaskTrade Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

TaskTrade (version 1) keeps a list of small errands and who owes what on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

No data leaves your phone

TaskTrade version 1 works fully offline. The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission and requests no other permissions, so it cannot send your tasks, photos or payment handles over the network. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

No real money is held or moved

TaskTrade version 1 does not hold, process or transfer money. Prices are records inside the app. When a task is done, the app can open Venmo or Cash App (or their web pages) with the runner's handle and the amount filled in. You then send the payment yourself in that app, and tell TaskTrade you sent it. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your tasks: title, details, category, price, status, history, and any payment handle you type in, in a private file inside the app.
  • A dispute photo, if you choose one. You pick it with an Android picker; the app copies it into its private storage. The app does not get access to your other photos.
  • Your look (theme) setting.

Android backup is turned off for TaskTrade, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing

TaskTrade does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, because it never leaves your phone. The only hand-off is the Venmo or Cash App link described above, which you start yourself.

Children

TaskTrade is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a later version adds an account, a server or real payments, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change.
