What Cheppify is

Cheppify turns your speech into text, in Telugu and English, and types it into the text field you are using. It works offline, on your phone. There is no account and no server, and your audio and text do not leave your phone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What the app uses, and why

  • Microphone: to hear what you say while you dictate. The recording is a temporary file that is deleted right after it is turned into text.
  • Accessibility service: to know which text field is focused and whether the keyboard is open, and to type the dictated words into that field. You turn it on yourself in Android Settings and can turn it off at any time. It does not read the text in your fields.
  • Display over other apps: to show Cheppify's small dictation bubble above other apps.

What is kept on your phone

Cheppify does not keep recordings. It keeps your last 30 dictations on the phone so you can copy one again, and your settings. You can delete the dictations any time with Clear transcript history in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Accuracy

Speech recognition makes mistakes, especially with background noise, accents, names, or mixed Telugu and English. Cheppify does not guarantee that the text is correct or complete. Check what it types before you send, save, or rely on it, particularly for anything important, such as medical, legal, financial, or safety matters.
