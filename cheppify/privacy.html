Cheppify Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short. Cheppify is a voice dictation app that works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no ads, so the developer never receives your voice, your text, or anything else about you. This page explains what the app handles on your phone while it works, and what you can do about it.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Cheppify app for Android (the "app") and to these pages. In this policy "we" and "the developer" mean the independent developer who publishes Cheppify. You can reach us using the contact address at the end.

2. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else

Cheppify works fully offline. The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your audio, your text, or any other information over the network. There is no account or sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting service, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect, receive, sell, share or transfer your personal information, because it never leaves your phone.

The speech model and the other software the app needs ship inside the app itself. Turning your voice into text happens on your phone, not on a server.

4. The permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone. To hear you while you dictate, as described above. It records only while a dictation is active.
  • Accessibility service. Cheppify uses an Android accessibility service for one purpose: dictation. It tells the app which text field is focused, what kind of field it is and whether the keyboard is open, so the bubble can appear at the right time, and it types the dictated words into the focused field when you tap the checkmark. It does not read what is written in any field, does not read your screen content, does not record what you type, and does not collect or store anything about other apps. You switch it on yourself in Android Settings and can switch it off at any time. The app explains this to you before it sends you there.
  • Display over other apps. Cheppify draws its small dictation bubble above other apps. It draws only its own bubble.
  • Notifications and foreground service. While the bubble is on, Android shows a persistent notification. This is how Android lets a microphone app keep working reliably in the background.

5. How long things are kept

Recordings are deleted right after they are turned into text. Dictation history keeps the last 30 dictations and removes older ones automatically. Settings and logs stay until you clear them or remove the app. Because we hold none of this data, there is nothing for us to keep on our side.

6. Security

Because your data never leaves your phone, the main protection is that there is nothing on a server to be breached. Data the app keeps is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read, and Android backup is turned off for Cheppify so this data is not copied to cloud backups. No method of storing data is completely secure, and the app does not add its own encryption on top of what Android provides, so protect your phone with a screen lock. Be aware that anything you dictate becomes ordinary text in the app you typed it into, and that app's own policies then apply to it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your history. Settings has a Clear transcript history button that deletes the stored dictations.
  • Remove everything. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • Turn permissions off. You can revoke the microphone permission, switch off the accessibility service, or remove the display-over-apps permission in Android Settings at any time. Dictation then stops working, but nothing else is affected.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection. Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California) the law may give you rights over your personal information. Since we do not collect or hold any of yours, there is nothing we can look up, but you are welcome to write to us with any request or question and we will answer it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information (including for advertising), and we do not use it to profile you or to make automated decisions about you.

8. Third-party software

Cheppify includes open-source components and a speech model that run on your phone and do not connect to the internet from the app: the Shruti Telugu speech model by Udgam Labs, the sherpa-onnx speech engine by the k2-fsa project, ONNX Runtime by Microsoft, and the Lottie animation library by Airbnb. They are listed with their licenses under About and licenses in Settings. When you open this policy or the Terms of Service from the app, your phone's web browser loads the page; the browser, not Cheppify, makes that connection, and its own privacy policy applies to it.

9. Children

Cheppify is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law of your country sets that age), and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about the app, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, we will update this page before that change ships, and the effective date above will change. If the app ever gains the ability to send data off your phone, this policy will say so first. Please check this page from time to time.
