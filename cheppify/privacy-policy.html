Cheppify Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Cheppify is a voice dictation app for Android. It turns your speech into text on your phone and types it into the text field you are using. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

No data leaves your phone

Cheppify works fully offline. The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your audio, your text, or anything else over the network. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

What the app uses, and why

  • Microphone. While you dictate, Cheppify records your voice to a temporary file in the app's private cache, turns it into text on the phone using a speech model that ships inside the app, and deletes the recording right after. The recording is not uploaded or shared.
  • Accessibility service. Cheppify uses an Android accessibility service for one purpose: to know which text field is focused and whether the keyboard is open, so it can show its dictation bubble at the right time and type the dictated words into that field. It does not read the text in any field, does not read your screen content, does not record what you type, and does not collect or store anything from other apps. You turn it on yourself in Android Settings and can turn it off at any time.
  • Display over other apps. Cheppify draws its small bubble above other apps so you can start dictating from any text field. It draws only its own bubble.
  • Notification and foreground service. While the bubble is on, Android shows a persistent notification. This is how Android lets a microphone app keep running reliably.
  • Clipboard. To insert text, Cheppify briefly places it on the clipboard (marked sensitive so keyboards do not keep it in their clipboard history), pastes it, then puts back what you had copied. If inserting fails, the text stays on the clipboard so you can paste it yourself.

What is stored on your phone

  • The last 30 dictations, so you can tap one to copy it again. They are not backed up and never leave the phone. Settings has a Clear transcript history button that deletes them.
  • Your settings (for example the output mode, the bubble look and size).
  • A local crash log, if the app crashes. It stays on the phone unless you choose Share crash log, which hands it to the app you pick.

Android backup is turned off for Cheppify, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing

Cheppify does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone, because it never leaves your phone.

Children

Cheppify is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the effective date above will change.
